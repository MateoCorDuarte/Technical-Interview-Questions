It is a RISC-based processor architecture.

*Cortex-A*: Processors for heavy OS, and third party applications.

*Cortex-R*: Aimed for  embedded processors, Real time signal processing and control applications.

*Cortex-M*: Microcontroller-oriented processors. Application-specific, or SoC uses.


*Cortex M0 & M0+*: Minimal cost, power, and area, simple sensing and controlling.


*Cortex M3*: Data-intense operations.


*Cortex M4 & 7:* Data-intense operations, DSP applications.
